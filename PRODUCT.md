# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
A family organising shared household tasks, presents, and medication for people and pets.

## Product Purpose
A shared interactive pinboard for everyday lists, family and pet medication logs, and Christmas and birthday gift planning.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Add and edit family members and pets in Settings. These are profiles, not separate login accounts.
- Create lists and complete tasks; plan gifts for each family member.
- Keep medication tracking and support both people and pets.
- Manage weekly recurring activities in a dedicated Weekly schedule: title, family/pet assignment (or everyone), weekdays, start time and optional end time. Keep a slim seven-day view without week navigation, an hourly grid, location, notes or a colour picker.
- Configure Medication, Weekly schedule, Lists & tasks and Presents pinboard summaries independently: choose shown details, filter for everyone or one profile, and pin or hide each summary while preserving its saved placement and size.
- Store activities, summary preferences and schedule-note dimensions as additive optional fields in the existing family board snapshot; existing boards remain compatible without a new table or column.
- Remove Dexcom integration. The API is unavailable to the user.
- Keep local development usable without signing in. Hosted household data remains private.
- Preserve existing medication records and migration history; do not destructively migrate shared data.
- Existing Next.js, Drizzle, Neon and Vercel repository; user handles commits and pushes.

## Brand Commitments
HealthHome remains the temporary name. The user approved a warm paper-style pinboard with movable cards and requested removal of the existing background image.
